The Story of AmisA

AmisA is more than a name.

Born from Amir and Golsa, it reflects a family's vision for excellence, craftsmanship, and meaningful relationships. The two capital A's symbolize the continuity of that vision through the next generation.

Inspired by the heritage of Shiraz and driven by a passion for dentistry, education, and innovation, AmisA was created to connect artistry, science, and human care.

May this small gift carry our appreciation for your dedication to creating healthy and confident smiles.

Current Scope

Current Scope of AmisA

AmisA Biodental Co. is currently focused on non-clinical dental education, continuing education programming, digital workflow support, and business-to-business dental laboratory case coordination for licensed dental professionals.

AmisA does not currently operate a dental clinic, provide patient care, diagnose conditions, develop patient treatment plans or perform restricted clinical activities in British Columbia.

Any future patient-care service would be established only after all required licences, permits and regulatory approvals have been obtained and would operate through appropriately licensed BCCOHP oral health professionals acting within their authorized scope of practice.

About

Research at AmisA

AmisA Biodental Co. supports research, innovation and academic collaboration across digital dentistry, prosthodontics, implant dentistry, dental materials, laboratory workflows and professional education.

Our goal is to connect clinical experience, digital technology, laboratory expertise and academic inquiry to support meaningful advancements in contemporary dentistry.

Focus Areas

Research Areas

01
Digital Dentistry

Digital workflows, intraoral scanning, CAD/CAM, digital treatment planning and emerging dental technologies.

02
Prosthodontics & Restorative Dentistry

Fixed and removable prosthodontics, ceramic restorations, veneers, treatment planning, occlusion and complex rehabilitation.

03
Implant Dentistry

Implant-supported restorations, digital implant workflows, prosthetic planning and interdisciplinary treatment concepts.

04
Dental Materials

Ceramics, zirconia, lithium disilicate, bonding systems, restorative materials and laboratory-related materials research.

05
Dental Laboratory Innovation

Digital laboratory workflows, CAD design, manufacturing processes, quality control and communication between clinicians and dental laboratories.

06
Dental Education

Clinical education, continuing education, assessment, simulation and innovative approaches to professional learning.

Working with AmisA LAB

Fast. Reliable. Design for Dentists. Estimated turnaround is confirmed for each case and may vary by restoration type, production requirements and destination.

Professional Use Only — Laboratory services are available only in response to prescriptions or authorized requests from licensed dental professionals. AmisA does not provide services directly to patients. Clinical assessment, tooth preparation, intraoral scanning, material selection, treatment decisions, delivery and follow-up remain the responsibility of the treating licensed professional. Fabrication is performed through appropriately qualified laboratory providers, subject to applicable jurisdictional requirements.

Section 03 · The Role

What You Will Do

Partners introduce AmisA services to dental professionals and support the growth of AmisA's international network. The role is relationship-driven and business-focused.

Partners do not perform dental procedures and do not need clinical knowledge. Partners must not provide diagnosis, clinical advice or treatment recommendations, and must not represent themselves as dentists, dental specialists or other regulated oral health professionals unless independently authorized to do so in their jurisdiction.

Responsibilities
Build professional relationships
Introduce AmisA services to dental professionals

English is helpful, but partners may work in their local language when appropriate.
