AmisA HUB — Account Deletion Request

This page explains how users of the AmisA HUB dental case management application, operated by AmisA Biodental, can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Email amisa.biodental.co@gmail.com with the subject AmisA HUB Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email address registered to your AmisA HUB account. Do not send passwords or patient records.
  3. We will contact you to verify account ownership and coordinate the deletion request.

Data covered by the request

Once your request is verified, we will review deletion of your account, profile information, and personal data associated with your use of AmisA HUB. Where applicable, data will be deleted or de-identified.

Data that may be retained

Some dental case records, transaction records, and security or audit logs may need to be retained to comply with applicable legal, clinical, contractual, or record-keeping requirements. We will explain any applicable retention in our response to your request.

Submitting a request does not immediately delete clinical or other records that must lawfully be retained.
