Privacy Policy — AmisA HUB Effective date: October 15, 2026 AmisA Biodental (“AmisA,” “we,” “us”) provides AmisA HUB, a dental laboratory case-management application for dental professionals. This policy explains how personal information is collected, used, disclosed and protected through the app and related services. Information we collect Depending on how the app is used, we may process dental professional contact and account details, patient names and identifiers, dental case instructions, treatment-related details needed for fabrication, intraoral photographs, digital dental scans (including STL files), order information, communications and technical information necessary to operate and secure the service. Dental professionals should provide only the minimum patient information needed for the requested service. How we use information We use information to manage accounts, receive and track orders, communicate with dental professionals, design and manufacture dental restorations or prostheses, coordinate delivery, provide support, maintain service security, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell patient information. Sharing and international processing To fulfill dental laboratory orders, relevant case information — potentially including patient names or identifiers, intraoral images, digital scans, and fabrication instructions — may be shared with authorized dental laboratory partners in China. Information may therefore be accessed, processed or stored outside Canada and may be subject to laws of those jurisdictions. We limit the case information shared to what is necessary for the requested services. We work with service providers to establish appropriate confidentiality and security safeguards; the specific safeguards depend on the services and agreements in place. We may also share information with hosting, technology, delivery and other service providers as needed to operate the service, or when legally required. Patient privacy and professional responsibilities Dental professionals submitting patient information should ensure they have the authority and any necessary patient notices or consents to disclose that information for laboratory services, including international transfers where applicable. AmisA handles information for the purposes described in this policy and in accordance with applicable arrangements with dental professionals. Security and retention We use administrative, technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, use and disclosure. The specific safeguards used may vary depending on the service and system involved. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Retention periods depend on the type of information, the purposes for which it is needed, and applicable legal or professional recordkeeping requirements. When information is no longer required, we take steps to delete or de-identify it as appropriate. Access, correction and deletion Dental professionals may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information, subject to identity verification, applicable laws, and necessary record-retention requirements. Requests concerning patient information may need to be coordinated with the treating dental professional. Where the app provides account creation, users may also request account deletion by contacting us using the details below. Children AmisA HUB is intended for dental professionals, not for use by children. Patient records may relate to minors when submitted by authorized dental professionals for legitimate laboratory services. Changes to this policy We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app, our practices or legal requirements. The current version will be posted at this page with an updated effective date. Contact For privacy questions or requests, contact AmisA Biodental at amisa.biodental.co@gmail.com.
